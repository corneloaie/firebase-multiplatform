FirebasePnvStatusCodes

object FirebasePnvStatusCodes(source)

The status codes of Phone Number Verification, the FirebasePhoneNumberVerificationException.errorCode of its failures; mirroring com.google.firebase.pnv.FirebasePnvStatusCodes. On Android the object also extends Google Play services' CommonStatusCodes, whose codes a failure can carry too.

Plain common code: on Android it is the SDK object, whose messages getMessage repeats on the other platforms.
